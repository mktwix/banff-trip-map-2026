Detailed Daily Plan (with Approximate Hours)

  • 21:30+: Rest up for tomorrow's drive into the mountains.

Sat Sep 12 — Drive West into the Rockies & Banff Town Exploration

  • 08:30 – 09:45: Complimentary hot breakfast at Hampton Inn · pack vehicle.
  • 10:00 – 11:30: Checkout Hampton Inn · scenic drive west along Trans-Canada Hwy 1 into the Rockies (~130 km, ~1 hr 20 min).
  • 11:30 – 12:00: Pass Banff Park East Gate · place Parks Canada Discovery Pass on vehicle dashboard.
  • 12:00 – 13:30: Arrive in Banff townsite · lunch in town (Bear Street Tavern, Nourish Bistro, or Whitebark Cafe).
  • 13:30 – 15:30: Explore Banff town on foot: stroll Banff Avenue, Cascade of Time Gardens, and walk along the Bow River trail down to Bow Falls viewpoint.
  • 15:30 – 16:30: Drive up to HI Banff Alpine Centre (801 Hidden Ridge Way on Tunnel Mountain; Ref 5164644914) · check in 2 beds in 4-bed mixed dorm, store bags.
  • 17:00 – 19:00: Late afternoon / sunset drive to Vermilion Lakes (docks with Mount Rundle reflection).
  • 19:30 – 21:30: Dinner in Banff town (Park Distillery or Eddie Burger + Bar) · return to HI Banff.

Sun Sep 13 — Johnston Canyon & Drive to Kootenay (Redstreak oTENTik)

  • 07:45 – 08:15: Coffee at hostel · 08:00 MDT (10:00 EDT) SHARP: Book Sep 15 Moraine Lake shuttle on reservation.pc.gc.ca (Last Minute release)!
  • 08:30 – 09:15: Checkout HI Banff Alpine Centre · pack car to beat crowds.
  • 09:15 – 09:45: Drive Bow Valley Parkway (Hwy 1A) to Johnston Canyon.
  • 09:45 – 12:30: Hike Johnston Canyon: suspended catwalks along canyon gorge, Lower Falls, and Upper Falls (~5 km return, ~2.5 hrs).
  • 12:30 – 13:15: Picnic lunch at Johnston Canyon day use area or Black Goat Cafe.
  • 13:15 – 15:30: Drive through Kootenay National Park to Radium via Castle Junction and Hwy 93 South (~135 km, ~2 hrs) · cross Continental Divide into BC.
  • 15:30 – 16:30: Check into Redstreak Campground oTENTik site A2 (INPC26-59887450B1) · unpack sleeping bags · note fire ban (no campfires/no cooking inside oTENTik).
  • 17:00 – 19:00: Soak in Radium Hot Springs Pools (walk-in ~$20/adult or scenic ~2 km trail from Redstreak H Loop).
  • 19:30 – 21:00: Dinner in Radium Hot Springs village (Horsethief Creek Pub or Bighorn Cafe) · relaxing camp night.

Mon Sep 14 — Kootenay to Banff & Tunnel Mountain Summit

  • 08:00 – 09:30: Breakfast in Radium village · (backup: 08:00 MDT / 10:00 EDT shuttle release for Sep 16 if Sep 15 missed).
  • 09:45 – 11:00: Pack up oTENTik A2 · checkout Redstreak by 11:00.
  • 11:00 – 13:15: Drive north through Kootenay along Hwy 93 · stop at Marble Canyon (~30 min walk over turquoise limestone gorge) and Continental Divide monument.
  • 13:30 – 14:45: Arrive Banff town · lunch at Three Bears Brewery or Chuck's Steakhouse.
  • 15:00 – 16:00: Check in HI Banff Alpine Centre (Ref 5825418201).
  • 16:30 – 18:30: Hike Tunnel Mountain Summit trail (trailhead near hostel · ~4.5 km return, 260m gain · panorama over Banff town and Bow Valley).
  • 19:00 – 21:00: Casual dinner in Banff town · early rest before tomorrow's early Moraine shuttle.

Tue Sep 15 — Moraine Lake, Lake Louise & Two Jack Lakeside oTENTik

  • 06:00 – 06:45: Early rise, pack car, grab coffee & quick breakfast.
  • 06:45 – 07:30: Drive Banff → Lake Louise Park and Ride (Lake Louise Ski Resort, ~40 mins).
  • 07:30 – 11:30: Moraine Lake (Primary Shuttle): board Parks Canada shuttle, climb Rockpile trail for the iconic "Twenty Dollar View" over the Ten Peaks, walk shoreline trail.
  • 11:30 – 14:00: Lake Louise Connector: free connector shuttle to Lake Louise · stroll lakeshore to back of lake or Fairview Lookout · lunch by the lake.
  • 14:00 – 14:45: Shuttle back to Park and Ride · drive east toward Banff.
  • 15:00 – 16:00: Check into Two Jack Lakeside Campground oTENTik Site 52 (INPC26-59538853B1) · prime waterfront site.
  • 17:00 – 19:00: Sunset at Lake Minnewanka (~5 min drive from campground) · shoreline walk, Stewart Canyon bridge, or relaxing scenic drive.
  • 19:30 – 21:00: Lakeside picnic or dinner in Banff town · stargazing at Two Jack Lake.

Wed Sep 16 — Two Jack to Tunnel Mountain V2 D17 (Scenic Transition & Rest)

  • 08:00 – 09:30: Relaxed morning coffee by Two Jack Lake · (backup: 06:00 AM shuttle if Moraine was moved to today).
  • 10:00 – 11:00: Pack up campsite · checkout Two Jack site 52 by 11:00.
  • 11:00 – 13:00: Scenic loop drive: Lake Minnewanka Loop, Bankhead Ghost Town trail, and Surprise Corner Viewpoint overlooking Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel.
  • 13:00 – 14:30: Lunch in Banff town · restock groceries, snacks, and trail water at Nesters Market.
  • 15:00 – 16:00: Check into Tunnel Mountain Village 2 oTENTik Site D17 (auto-paid reservation).
  • 16:30 – 18:30: Easy afternoon: walk the Hoodoos Trail along Bow River canyon, or rent a canoe at Banff Canoe Club.
  • 19:00 – 21:00: Dinner in Banff town · fill gas tank tonight for tomorrow's full-day Icefields Parkway journey.

Thu Sep 17 — Full Day Icefields Parkway (Bow Lake, Peyto Lake, Columbia Icefield)

  • 07:30 – 08:30: Early breakfast, prep packed lunch and thermos, verify full gas tank.
  • 08:30 – 10:00: Drive north along Hwy 1 and onto Hwy 93 North (Icefields Parkway), one of the world's most scenic alpine highways.
  • 10:00 – 10:45: Bow Lake: stop at historic Num-Ti-Jah Lodge · serene turquoise waters reflecting Crowfoot and Bow Glaciers.
  • 11:00 – 12:30: Peyto Lake & Bow Summit: short walk to the famous wolf-head shaped viewing deck · highest highway point in the park.
  • 12:30 – 13:45: Drive north past Big Hill and Parker Ridge to the Columbia Icefield (Athabasca Glacier).
  • 13:45 – 15:30: Walk the Toe of the Athabasca Glacier trail (touch glacial breezes, see recession markers) · picnic lunch overlooking the glacier.
  • 15:30 – 17:30: Scenic return drive south towards Banff in afternoon golden light · optional stops at Mistaya Canyon or Waterfowl Lakes.
  • 17:30 – 18:30: Return to Tunnel Mountain Village 2 · check into Site D5 (INPC26-60395520B1).
  • 19:00 – 21:00: Hearty dinner in Banff town (The Bison Restaurant, Brazen, or Three Bears).

Fri Sep 18 — Tunnel Mountain checkout, HI Banff check-in & Banff Gondola / Hot Springs

  • 08:30 – 10:00: Pack camping gear into Civic · checkout Tunnel Mountain Village 2 (D5) by 11:00.
  • 10:30 – 11:30: Drive across Tunnel Mountain to HI Banff Alpine Centre (Ref 5571085239) · drop luggage in secure storage before room check-in.
  • 11:30 – 13:00: Lunch in Banff town (Tooloulou's, Banff Ave, or Whitebark Cafe).
  • 13:30 – 15:30: Banff Gondola (Sulphur Mountain):
    • Ride the 8-minute glass gondola to the summit of Sulphur Mountain (2,281m / 7,486 ft).
    • Walk the multi-level ridge boardwalk to the historic Sanson's Peak Cosmic Ray Station for 360° views across six mountain ranges and Bow Valley.
    • Explore the Above Banff interactive interpretive center and rooftop observation deck.
  • 15:45 – 17:30: Banff Upper Hot Springs: Walk directly next door from the Gondola terminal (shares the same parking lot!) and soak in the outdoor thermal mineral pools with views across the valley.
  • 18:00 – 19:30: Return to HI Banff · check into dorm rooms · late afternoon walk to Bow Falls or sunset at Vermilion Lakes.
  • 19:30 – 21:30: Celebratory dinner in Banff town (Park Distillery or Maclab Bistro at Banff Centre with mountain terrace views).

Sat Sep 19 — Canmore & Kananaskis Country Focus, 21:30 Turo Return

  • (Note: Purchase 1-Day Kananaskis Conservation Pass online for ~$15+tax per vehicle using your Civic license plate before arrival).
  • 08:30 – 09:30: Pack dorm beds, checkout HI Banff Alpine Centre · stash all luggage securely out of sight in the Civic trunk.
  • 09:30 – 10:00: Drive east down Hwy 1 to Canmore (~20 mins).
  • 10:00 – 10:45: Morning artisan coffee and breakfast in Canmore (Eclipse Coffee Roasters or Rocky Mountain Bagel Co.).
  • 11:00 – 13:00: Grassi Lakes Trail: hike the ~4 km scenic loop (~2 hrs). Take the "More Difficult" interpretive trail going up (waterfall views, stone stairs, views across Bow Valley & Ha Ling Peak) and gentle road descent · admire twin turquoise pools.
  • 13:15 – 14:45: Downtown Canmore: walk across historic wooden Engine Bridge over Bow River (Mount Rundle views) · lunch on 8th Street (Grizzly Paw Pub or Communitea Café).
  • 15:00 – 17:30: Kananaskis Country Scenic Exploration:
    • Option A (Gravel Mountain Drive): Smith-Dorrien Trail (Hwy 742) past Whitemans Pond, Goat Pond, and Spray Lakes Reservoir (crisp mountain reflection, golden autumn larches).
    • Option B (Smooth Paved Drive): Hwy 1 to Hwy 40 South to Barrier Lake shoreline & Wedge Pond.
  • 17:30 – 19:30: Farewell dinner in Canmore (Bridgette Bar Canmore, Crazyweed, or Tavern 1883).
  • 19:30 – 20:45: Drive from Canmore to Calgary Airport (YYC) via Hwy 1 and Stoney Trail NW (~115 km, ~1 hr 15 min).
  • 20:45 – 21:30: Gas fill-up near YYC (Airport Trail NE / 96 Ave NE) · shake out floor mats · take Turo checkout photos (odometer, fuel gauge, exterior/interior).
  • 21:30: Turo Honda Civic Return at YYC.
  • 21:45: Walk into YYC Terminal (Concourse A/B) · drop checked 23kg camping gear bag with Flair Airlines for flight F8 628 · clear security with plenty of unhurried time.
